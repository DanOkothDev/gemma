Hello, and welcome to RxGuard

Make more informed decisions about your medication.

We're glad you're here. RxGuard helps you review medication information, understand potential interactions [when two medicines affect each other], explore food cautions, and organise your daily schedule in one friendly place.

RxGuard provides informational support, not a diagnosis or a replacement for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
